Our Heritage & Scale

South Bengaluru's Grand Destination for Fancy & Plastic Ware

Mayur Fancy and Gift Plastic House was founded on a simple yet ambitious vision: households in Konanakunte and surrounding neighborhoods should never have to travel across Bengaluru or visit multiple shops to prepare for a family festival, furnish their kitchen, or buy celebration gifts.

What started as a specialty novelty store has expanded over decades into a monumental superstore. From sturdy, heavy-duty plastic storage bins, buckets, and airtight kitchen containers to opulent wedding return gifts, bridal bangles, student backpacks, and trending toys — our aisles are stocked deep with wholesale-competitive value.

Whether you need a single stylish gift wrapped for an evening birthday party, or 200 return gift hampers for a wedding or housewarming ceremony, Mayur delivers with warm personalized assistance and unbeatable prices.
