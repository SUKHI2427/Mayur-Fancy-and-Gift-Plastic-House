Our story

A neighbourhood shop that grew by being useful, one gift at a time.

Mayur Fancy and Gift Plastic House started with a simple idea: families in Konanakunte should not have to travel far for a birthday gift, a festival decoration or a sturdy set of plastic ware.

Over the years the shelves grew from a few fancy items to a full range of gifts, toys, accessories, kitchen and storage ware. Regular customers still walk in for one thing and leave with three.

We know most of our customers by name, and we are happy to help you choose.
